Frequently asked questions.

What is TeslaStream?
TeslaStream is a free IPTV player that works in your Tesla, EV, or any web browser. It connects to your Xtream Codes IPTV provider and plays live TV, movies, and series directly — no app install needed.
Do I need an IPTV subscription?
Yes — you need credentials from an IPTV provider (server URL, username, password). TeslaStream is the player; the content comes from your provider. We also include sample channels to try without any subscription.
Does this work in my Tesla or EV's browser?
Yes. TeslaStream is specifically built for Tesla and EV browsers. It uses a canvas-based renderer that bypasses the driving playback restriction — you can watch while parked or charging.
Do I need to install anything?
No. TeslaStream is a web app — open it in any browser and start watching. On Tesla, you can add it to your home screen for a full-screen app icon.
Is my information safe?
Yes. Everything is stored locally in your browser — nothing is sent to our servers. Your credentials stay on your device.
